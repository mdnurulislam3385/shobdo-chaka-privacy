Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Shobdo Chaka (শব্দ চাকা) is a Bangla word puzzle game developed by Rifat Hossain. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

Without an account: the game saves coins, level, hints, themes and settings only on your device.

With an account (optional): if you create an account or sign in with email, we store your email address and an account ID through Firebase Authentication. Your game progress (coins, level, hints, themes and purchase history) is stored in Cloud Firestore under your account ID so it can sync between devices.

Purchases: coin packs and themes are bought through Google Play Billing. We receive purchase confirmations from Google Play; we do not receive your payment card details.

Support: if you contact us through the in-app Support option (phone or WhatsApp), we receive only what you send us.

How we use it

We use this information to run the game, keep your progress across devices, restore your purchases, and answer support requests. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

Firebase (Google) provides sign-in and cloud storage. Google Play handles purchases. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they process.

Keeping and deleting data

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app: open the menu, tap Delete account. This deletes your account and the cloud copy of your progress. Progress stored only on your phone stays there until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

You can also request deletion by contacting us as described below.

Children

The app is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described above.

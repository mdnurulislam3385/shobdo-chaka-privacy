Delete your Shobdo Chaka account

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Shobdo Chaka and tap the menu icon on the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to log in again first, log out, sign in with the same email, and repeat the steps.

Request deletion by contact

If you cannot open the app, email rifarrana7@gmail.com from the email linked to the account and ask for deletion, or call or WhatsApp 01635556222.

What is deleted

Your account login (email and account ID) and the cloud copy of your game progress, coins, themes and purchase history.

Progress stored only on your phone is not removed by this step. Clear the app data or uninstall the app to remove it.
